How it works

From a company name to a signed deal, in three pages.

Research fills the list. Today works it. Conversations closes it. The screenshots show invented companies and people.

01 · Research

Facts with the link they came from

Say who you're looking for and send an agent after them. Ask for about so many companies and watch how far the search has got. What it finds that you already have is left out, so the queue holds only what is new.

Sell more than one thing? Each offering gets its own look-for list and queue, so a company that is wrong for one can still be right for another. Every fact keeps its source.

02 · Today

One queue, best first

Everything due across LinkedIn, email and phone, in this order:

  1. Conversations that need a next step
  2. Calls where it's office hours for the contact
  3. LinkedIn messages, emails and follow-ups
  4. LinkedIn adds

03 · Conversations

Replies become deals

Replies move across a board: replied, meeting, proposal, won, lost.

Each one needs a next step with a date. Any without one, or due today, come back to the top of Today.

After it's won

The customer stays on the same record.

Deals, the contract, what they're on, getting them live, what they write in about and what you write to them, all on the company you prospected. Each has its operations in the published API.

Deals with a value

Each conversation carries what it's worth, who has it and when it should close. Start a second one for a renewal or an expansion.

Agreements, ready to sign

Upload the contract as a PDF. It is kept exactly as uploaded and checked against its hash on every download. Choose who signs and in what order, and drag their fields onto the pages. Sending for signature comes next.

Subscriptions and their notice

What each customer is on, for how long, and the notice it needs. Today flags one a month before its date, and a renewal deal starts from it.

Onboarding to go-live

Your steps and theirs, dated from the start. The customer gets a page of their own steps from an emailed link, no password, and marks theirs done. Late steps come back to Today.

Enquiries answered

Site forms and your product's sign-ups arrive open, to be given an owner. Reply by email from the card, and paste their answer into the thread.

Campaigns to people you know

Write once to everyone in conversation or at a current customer, never to cold prospects. It goes through your own mail server with your postal address and a one-click unsubscribe, and nobody is mailed twice. A service notice, such as a price change, reaches customers who unsubscribed from marketing.

Agents and API

Agents do the reading. You decide what they may send.

Each organisation has an MCP endpoint and a REST API with scoped keys. Connect Claude or your own agent in one command, and decide what each key may do. Or let Prospector run the agents itself, through Claude, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint or a model you host, with free search from your own SearXNG. Each agent's card says whether it is ready, and why not.

Built for a small team

The details that keep a list clean.

A never-contact list that holds

Domains, companies, email addresses and LinkedIn profiles. A never-contact company stays on file but out of review and outreach. It applies to agents, imports and edits alike.

Calls at a sensible hour

Office hours come from the contact's phone number, so Zurich gets called when Zurich is at work.

The full history

Every company shows who did what and when, people and agents alike.

Several organisations, one login

Each has its own prospects, wording, team and keys. None can see another's. Sign in with a password, Google, Microsoft, LinkedIn or your organisation's own single sign-on.

Bring your own files

Import companies from CSV and drafts from markdown. Re-importing never clears a date or an edit made in the app.

Your data stays yours

Host it yourself on SQLite or Postgres, or let us. An owner can download everything the organisation holds as one file, and erase a person who asks to be forgotten.
